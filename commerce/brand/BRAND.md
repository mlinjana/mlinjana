# Mlinjana Studios brand

Logo **D · Folded page**, chosen by the owner on 5 Nov 2026. Palette: **Midnight Navy, Warm Ivory and Champagne Gold**, chosen on 6 Nov 2026 for Mlinjana Studios 2.0 (DECISIONS.md #39). It replaces the earlier Emerald & Marigold; the logo shape is unchanged.
All artwork is original vector work generated by `brand/make_logo.py` (wordmark outlines from Plus Jakarta Sans, SIL OFL 1.1).

## Logo files (`brand/logo/final/`, served from `/assets/brand/`)
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo.svg` / `logo.png` | Main logo on white or light backgrounds |
| `logo-reversed.svg` / `logo-reversed.png` | On Midnight Navy, dark or photo backgrounds |
| `icon.svg`, `icon-16/32/48/180/192/512.png`, `favicon.ico` | Symbol alone: browser tab, app icon, social profile |
| `apple-touch-icon.png` | iPhone home screen (solid background) |
| `og.png` | Link previews (WhatsApp, Facebook, X, LinkedIn): 1200 × 630 |

Rebuild everything: `python3 -c "import sys; sys.path.insert(0,'brand'); import make_logo as m; m.export_final()"` then `node brand/render_png.js`, then copy into `engine/static/brand/`. `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png` (on white) and `engine/static/brand/logo-email.png` (316 × 84, on white, for receipt emails) are made from those PNGs with Pillow.

## Using the logo
- **The idea:** a bold M on a page with its corner folded over in gold: documents and templates, made with care. The dots on the *i* and *j* in "Mlinjana" are gold squares, a detail that ties the wordmark to the symbol.
- **Clear space:** keep space around the logo at least the height of the "M" in the symbol.
- **Minimum size:** full logo 120 px wide on screens (25 mm in print); the symbol alone down to 16 px.
- **Don't:** stretch or squash it, recolour the M or the fold, put the navy version on a navy background (use the reversed version), add shadows or outlines, or rearrange the symbol and wordmark.
