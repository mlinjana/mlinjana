How it works

From manuscript to bookshop in four steps

  1. 1

    Create an author account

    Sign in with Google or your email, then add your bio and payout details.

  2. 2

    Submit your manuscript

    Upload your file, cover (or ask for one), description, genre and language.

  3. 3

    Editorial review

    Our editors check quality, originality and our content rules, and reply with "approved" or notes to fix.

  4. 4

    Go on sale and get paid

    Approved books go live. You see every sale in your portal and are paid monthly.
