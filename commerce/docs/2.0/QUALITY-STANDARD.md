# The Mlinjana standard: what every product must contain

Owner direction, 7 Nov 2026: "if a product is expensive, the product must be worth the money ... even the cheap ones,
even the free ones ... if need be we even give an example." No product is sold above what its content justifies.
`engine/value_audit.py` measures every file and writes `docs/2.0/value-audit.csv`. Its scoring ladder is our own
judgement, used to rank products and set ceilings, not a market benchmark.

## Every product
- Solves one clear job, named in the title, with nothing padded or repeated (no copy-pasted blank sections).
- Shows the buyer a **finished, realistic South African example**, not only empty boxes.
- Explains how to use it in plain language, inside the file.
- Spelling and facts checked; legal, tax and labour points stay general and tell the buyer to confirm with an adviser.

## Word templates (.docx)
1. Short guide: when to use it and how to fill it in well, with before-and-after examples where useful.
2. The editable template, with grey guidance the buyer deletes.
3. At least one **fully worked example** (complete, realistic content: names, amounts, steps).
4. Supporting pieces the job really needs (checklists, registers, sign-off sheets).
5. Tables use fixed column widths so they print properly in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice.

## Spreadsheets (.xlsx)
1. "Start Here" sheet with steps and what each sheet does.
2. Example data that shows every feature working, plus a clean blank version or a one-step way to clear it.
3. Real calculation logic, checked with independently calculated values (current factory check), protected formula cells.
4. Drop-downs and input checks where typing errors are likely; a summary or dashboard with at least one chart.

## Printables (.pdf)
1. Instructions page, A4 and US Letter.
2. A filled-in example page next to the blank pages.
3. Enough pages for real use (for example a year, a term or a full project), not a single repeated page.

## Model product
SOP Template Kit v2 (BZW-008), 7 Nov 2026: about 680 words before, 6,036 words, 56 tables and 30 pages after.
It has a writing guide, master and one-page templates, 11 complete worked SOPs (opening, cash-up, online orders,
receiving stock, complaints, refunds, client onboarding, a new employee's first week, month-end, load-shedding, data
backup) and an SOP register, training sign-off and review schedule. Audit score 16 → 82 (fair ceiling R129–R149;
price R149).
