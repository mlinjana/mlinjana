## Seven-day organic acquisition experiment

- Day 1: Run an approved full-price purchase, confirm receipt and download delivery. Confirm the live launch URL, mobile checkout and new funnel dashboard. Do not promote before this gate.
- Day 2: Film 20–30-second demos of the actual stock and invoice tools. Record a real screen while opening the actual file and completing one representative task. Avoid fabricated outcomes.
- Day 3: Publish the stock demo on the business' Facebook/LinkedIn pages and relevant communities that allow business promotion. Link to the exact product.
- Day 4: Publish the invoicing demo. Add a genuine FAQ answer about Excel requirements and download access; avoid suggesting a product feature that the file has not been tested to provide.
- Day 5: Publish one budgeting demo and one teacher-planner demonstration. Compare which audience generates genuine product views, basket additions and purchases.
- Day 6: Personally contact 10–15 relevant small-business or professional contacts **only where a legitimate relationship or permission exists**. Offer a sample and ask for practical feedback, not fake public reviews.
- Day 7: Review channel-level visitor activity, checkout failures, paid orders and verified files delivered. Keep the two strongest demonstrations; improve the landing page if interest doesn't turn into basket additions.

## Demonstration format (20–30 seconds)

1. 0–4s: On-screen problem statement, such as “Still updating stock counts in a notebook?”
2. 4–18s: Record **real** spreadsheet/document contents; show one practical action. Hide personal details and financial data.
3. 18–25s: Show the product page and its real preview/format and listed price. Say who it might help.
4. 25–30s: “See the actual files and details at Mlinjana Studios.” Use the matching product URL.

## Measurement and stop/go rules

These are working targets, not established performance benchmarks. Starting goal: five unrelated full-price customers who successfully receive their files. Track:
- Product views and basket additions by selected product;
- Checkout starts, handoffs, and errors;
- Verified live payments **excluding owner tests**;
- Payment success, receipt and download support incidents;
- Acquisition spend and contribution margin if paid campaigns begin.

Avoid interpreting aggregate events as unique buyers. Do not estimate conversion from raw Cloudflare page loads. Before any paid advertising, validate purchase flow and establish clean paid-order reporting. Paid campaigns require separate owner budget approval.

## First support feedback questions

After a genuine purchase, seek consent before contacting a customer for feedback. Ask:
1. Did the download arrive and open correctly?
2. Did the product description match the actual file?
3. What was confusing about buying or using it?
4. What additional tool would be useful?

Do not publish identifiable feedback or customer names without consent.
