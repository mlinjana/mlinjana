# What people search for: demand research (5 Nov 2026)

Goal: only add products people are actually looking for. This file records the evidence, how strong it is, and the resulting build order.

## How this was researched (and the limits)

| Source | What it tells us | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Google autocomplete (suggestqueries.google.com, `gl=za` and `gl=us`, 5 Nov 2026) | The phrases real people commonly type after a seed word, e.g. "worksheets for" → "grade r", "grade 1"… Autocomplete only shows queries with meaningful search activity. | **No volumes.** Order is Google's, not a ranking by volume. Raw output: not committed (re-run any time). |
| Optimsy "Digital Products to Sell in 2026" (measured 1 Oct–4 Nov 2026 from Etsy's live search) | Etsy listing counts (= competition/market size, **not** demand), median price, and how many of the top 100 results are under 30 days old (= how easily new listings get seen). | One vendor's tool; listing count is supply, not buyers. |
| Seller-blog round-ups (Outfy, LitCommerce, InsightAgent, etc.) | Which categories sellers report as selling. | Affiliate/vendor content; rankings disagree; growth/margin figures unverified. |
| Google Year in Search 2025, South Africa (via news coverage) | Broad SA interests (recipes, "how to" queries). | Not product-specific; secondary coverage. |
| Takealot home page (screenshot) | Layout patterns: big search, wishlist, department + deals bar. | UX only. Etsy and Shein blocked automated access, so they were not viewed. |

Not available without an account or payment (so not used): Google Trends (refused automated requests, HTTP 429), Google Keyword Planner, eRank/EverBee/Marmalead. Real volumes would need one of these (owner decision, see TASKS).

## Evidence: strong, specific search phrases (South Africa autocomplete)

- **Calendars:** "printable calendar 2026 south africa", "calendar 2027 south africa with public holidays", "… with school holidays", "… pdf", "… excel". Timely now (Nov–Jan).
- **Kids' worksheets (Grade R–4):** "worksheets for grade r / grade 1 … grade 4", "grade r worksheets term 3/4", "… afrikaans", "… maths", "phonics worksheets grade r–3", "handwriting practice for grade 1", "times tables worksheets pdf", "isizulu worksheets grade 1–7", "afrikaans worksheets grade 1–10".
- **Colouring:** "colouring pages for kids / adults / toddlers / girls / boys / teens", "colouring pages pdf".
- **Puzzles (adults and seniors):** "word search for adults printable", "… large print", "… hard", "sudoku printable with answers", "… 4 per page", "… easy/hard". "activities for elderly with dementia", "dementia activities printable".
- **SA life events:** "funeral programme template word / south africa / in zulu", "tombstone unveiling programme template word / pdf south africa".
- **SA admin:** "payslip template south africa (excel/word)", "payslip template for domestic worker", "invoice template south africa", "quotation template word/excel", "minutes of meeting template", "business plan template south africa", "stokvel constitution", "budget template south africa excel", "cv template south africa / for students with no experience".
- **Study:** "study timetable for grade 12 / 11 / 10 / 9", "matric study guides" (official past papers are free from DBE; don't sell those).
- **Home and family:** "chore chart / reward chart for kids printable", "… in afrikaans", "meal plan template", "recipe card template", "habit / mood / debt / savings / bill / medication tracker printable".
- **Parties:** "baby shower games printable", "birthday party printable invitations", "wedding invitation templates (editable)".
- **Faith:** "bible study printables for women / kids", "large print bible".
- Note: many top phrases include **"free"**. A few genuinely free downloads (e.g. a basic 2027 SA calendar) can bring these searchers to the site; paid versions add editability, planners and extras.

## Etsy signal (Optimsy, Oct–Nov 2026)

New listings reach the top 100 most easily in: coloring book pdf (75 of 100 under 30 days), home planner (68), printable games (66), printable planner (51), digital planner (41). Wedding templates have the highest median price ($12.36). Treat as competition data, not demand.

## Build order (highest evidence × feasibility × timeliness first)

1. **2027 South Africa calendars and planners** (PDF + Excel; public holidays computed from the Public Holidays Act; school terms only from the official DBE calendar). Time-sensitive. One free basic version.
2. **Puzzles & activities shop:** large-print word searches, sudoku with answers, mazes, kids' activity packs, dementia-friendly activity packs. Code-generated and machine-checkable (e.g. each sudoku has exactly one solution).
3. **Kids' learning shop (Grade R–3):** phonics, handwriting, numbers, times tables, alphabet colouring. English first; Afrikaans and isiZulu only after review by a fluent speaker.
4. **Life events shop:** funeral programme and tombstone unveiling programme templates (Word), memorial pages, baby shower games, kids' party kits.
5. **Additions to existing shops:** SA payslip incl. domestic worker (UIF); stokvel tracker and constitution template; matric study timetables; "CV template South Africa" wording on existing career products.
6. **Colouring shop:** code-generated mandalas, patterns and simple kids' pages. Wall art later, only if quality holds up.
7. **Faith:** bible study journals (public-domain verse text only). Owner decision.

Rules for every new product: no trademarked characters or brands, no copied content, no "CAPS-aligned"/"approved" claims unless checked against the official CAPS document, no fake reviews or urgency, validated before listing (same as the 600).
