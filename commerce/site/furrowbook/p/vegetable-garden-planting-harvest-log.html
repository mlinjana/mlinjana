What's inside

  • Record what you sowed, where and when, and how much you harvested.
  • 'Planting' log (300 rows): Date, Crop, Variety, Bed, Method, Quantity, Expected harvest
  • 'Harvest' log (300 rows): Date, Crop, Bed, Weight kg, Quality
  • Automatic weight kg totals by crop, with chart
  • Key numbers: Harvested kg, Plantings
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works

How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Planting' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'Harvest' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  5. Delete the example rows before you start.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
