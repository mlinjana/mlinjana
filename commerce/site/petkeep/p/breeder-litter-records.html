How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Litter' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'Homes' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.

Works with
