Item details
  • Record farrier, worming, vet, feed and riding for each horse.
  • 'Care' log (300 rows): Date, Horse, Type, Provider, Cost, Next due
  • 'Riding' log (300 rows): Date, Horse, Activity, Minutes
  • Automatic cost totals by type, with chart
  • Key numbers: Care costs
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works
How to use it
  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Care' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'Riding' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  5. Delete the example rows before you start.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
