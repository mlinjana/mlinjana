Item details
  • 500 SKUs, 5,000 movement rows
  • On hand, stock value and reorder flag per item
  • Dashboard: total value, items to reorder, value by category
  • Drop-downs and editable lists
How to use it
  1. Add each product on 'Items' with SKU, cost, price, opening stock and reorder level.
  2. Record every stock movement on 'Movements': In (purchases/returns), Out (sales/usage) or Adjust (counts, damage; +/-).
  3. 'Items' calculates current stock, stock value and flags items at or below the reorder level.
  4. 'Dashboard' shows total stock value, items to reorder and value by category.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
