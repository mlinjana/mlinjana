How to use it

  1. List up to 6 people on 'People' and their share weight (e.g. 1 each to split equally, or 60/40 by income).
  2. Log each shared expense on 'Expenses': who paid, the amount and whether to split by the default weights or equally.
  3. 'Balances' shows what each person paid, their fair share and who owes whom.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
