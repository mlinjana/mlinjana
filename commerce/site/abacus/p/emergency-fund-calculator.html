How to use it

  1. List your essential monthly costs on 'Calculator' — the things you must pay even if income stops.
  2. Choose how many months of cover you want (3–6 is a common guideline; choose what fits your situation).
  3. Enter what you have saved and what you can add each month to see your target, gap and timeline.
  4. Log contributions on 'Tracker' to watch your months of cover grow.
