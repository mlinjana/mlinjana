What's included

  • 12-week usage history per SKU
  • Safety stock from service level and demand variability
  • Reorder point and EOQ rounded to pack size
  • 'Order now' alerts and days of stock

How to use

  1. On 'Usage History' enter the units used/sold per week for each SKU (up to 12 weeks).
  2. On 'Reorder Plan' enter lead time, stock on hand, stock on order, order cost, holding cost and pack size, and your service level.
  3. The sheet calculates average demand, safety stock, reorder point and economic order quantity (EOQ), and tells you what to order now.
