Item details
  • Weekly receipts and payments by category
  • Rolling closing cash
  • Buffer warnings and lowest-cash summary
  • Closing cash chart
How to use it
  1. Enter the opening bank balance, the first week's start date and a minimum cash buffer.
  2. Fill in expected receipts and payments per week (yellow cells). Rename rows to match your business.
  3. Closing cash rolls forward each week; weeks below your buffer are highlighted in red.
  4. Update it weekly: replace the past week's forecast with actuals and add a new week at the end.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
