How to use it

  1. At the end of each month enter totals (not individual transactions) on 'Income', 'Spending' and 'Saving'.
  2. 'Year Summary' calculates your savings rate, average spending and biggest categories, with charts.
  3. Use 'Money Goals' at the start of the year and review it each quarter.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
