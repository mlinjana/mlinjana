How to use it

  1. 'Repayment': enter the property price, deposit, rate and term to see the monthly repayment and total interest.
  2. 'Rate Changes': see how the repayment moves if the interest rate rises or falls.
  3. 'Affordability': enter your gross income and the share of income you're comfortable spending to see an estimated maximum loan.
  4. 'Extra Payments': see how paying more each month shortens the loan.
