How to use it

  1. Set targets per activity on 'Targets' (units per hour and accuracy).
  2. Each shift, log one row per operator per activity on 'Daily Log': units, lines/orders, hours and errors.
  3. 'Dashboard' summarises any date range: units per hour by operator and activity, accuracy, and status vs target.
