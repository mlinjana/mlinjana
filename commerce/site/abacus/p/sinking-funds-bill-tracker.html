What's included

How to use

  1. List every bill on 'Bills' with amount, how often it's paid and the month it's next due.
  2. The sheet converts each bill to a monthly equivalent and builds a 12-month bill calendar.
  3. On 'Sinking Funds', list future costs (car service, school fees, holidays) — the monthly amount to set aside calculates.
  4. Tick bills off on 'Paid Tracker' each month.
