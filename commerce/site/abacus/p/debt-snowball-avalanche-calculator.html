What's included

  • Up to 10 debts, 30-year month-by-month simulation
  • Switch between snowball and avalanche to compare
  • Payoff order and date for every debt
  • Total interest and debt-free date
  • Warnings if minimums don't cover interest or exceed your budget

How to use

  1. List up to 10 debts on 'Debts': balance, annual interest rate and minimum monthly payment.
  2. Enter the total amount you can pay towards debt each month and choose a method: Snowball (smallest balance first) or Avalanche (highest interest first).
  3. 'Schedule' simulates every month: minimums are paid on all debts and the extra goes to the priority debt. When a debt is cleared, its payment rolls over to the next.
  4. Switch the method to compare debt-free date and total interest. 'Summary' shows the payoff order and dates.
