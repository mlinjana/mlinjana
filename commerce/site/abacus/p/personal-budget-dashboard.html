How to use it

  1. On 'Budget', set a monthly budget for each category (rename categories on 'Lists').
  2. Record spending and income on 'Transactions' as it happens (or weekly from your bank statement).
  3. 'Dashboard' shows budget vs actual for the month you pick, with remaining amounts and % used.
  4. 'Year' shows actual spending by category for all 12 months with a chart.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
