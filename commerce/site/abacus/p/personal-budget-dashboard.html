Personal Budget Dashboard

AAbacus Lane

Set a budget per category, log transactions, and see budget vs actual, remaining money and savings rate for any month.

  • Digital download · 31 KB ZIP
  • XLSX

Instant download. Your files appear as soon as PayFast confirms payment. Checkout runs on PayFast's secure page.

Item details
  • 17 editable categories with a 12-month budget grid
  • 2,000-row transaction log with drop-downs
  • Monthly dashboard: budget vs actual, % used, savings rate
  • Year view by category with chart
How to use it
  1. On 'Budget', set a monthly budget for each category (rename categories on 'Lists').
  2. Record spending and income on 'Transactions' as it happens (or weekly from your bank statement).
  3. 'Dashboard' shows budget vs actual for the month you pick, with remaining amounts and % used.
  4. 'Year' shows actual spending by category for all 12 months with a chart.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
