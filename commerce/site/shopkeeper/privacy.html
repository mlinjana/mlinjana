Privacy

We collect as little personal information as possible.

What we collect

  • Orders: your email address, the product, amount, date and payment status. We need these to deliver your files and keep tax records.
  • Payments: handled entirely by PayFast. We never see or store your card or bank details. PayFast's own privacy policy applies to the payment step.
  • Messages: what you send through the contact form.
  • Visit statistics: page path, product viewed, and the website that referred you. No cookies, no IP addresses, no advertising trackers.

How we use it

Only to deliver your order, provide support, keep business records and understand which products are useful. We don't sell or share your information for marketing, and we don't send newsletters unless you ask to join one.

Where it's stored

Order data is stored with our hosting provider (Supabase, EU data centre) with access restricted to the store owner.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (we may need to keep order records for tax law). Use the contact form. South African customers may also contact the Information Regulator.
