About Shopkeeper's Desk

Sales and stock trackers, pricing calculators, policies and customer-service templates for online and market sellers.

Made for etsy sellers, online shops, makers and market traders. Shopkeeper's Desk is a studio of Mlinjana Capital Holdings (Pty) Ltd.

What you can expect

  • Every spreadsheet is recalculated and checked for formula errors before release
  • Printables come in A4 and US Letter
  • Word templates open in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice
  • Help within 14 days if a file doesn't work for you

Studio policies: Refunds · Licence · Terms · Privacy · Contact

Questions

How do I get my files?

Once PayFast confirms your payment, your download buttons appear on the confirmation page. Each link works 5 times over 7 days. Save the ZIP somewhere safe.

Can I buy several items at once?

Yes. Add them to your basket and pay once. Every item gets its own download link.

Will it work on my computer or phone?

Spreadsheets work in Microsoft Excel (2010 or later), Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Word templates open in Word, Google Docs and LibreOffice. PDFs open in any PDF reader. Each listing says exactly what it works with.

Can I use it for my business?

Yes, for yourself and for one business you own or work for. You can't resell or share the files. See the licence.

What if a file doesn't work?

Contact us within 14 days with your order number. We'll help, send a fixed file or refund you. See refunds & support.

Need help with an order?

Send us a message with your order number. We reply by email, usually within two working days.
