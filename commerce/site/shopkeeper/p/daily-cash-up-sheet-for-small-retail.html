What's inside

  • Reconcile tills daily: float, cash, card and variances.
  • 'Cash-up' log (300 rows): Date, Till, Float, Cash counted, Card total, Till report total, Variance…
  • Key numbers: Total variance, Card sales
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works

How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Cash-up' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.
