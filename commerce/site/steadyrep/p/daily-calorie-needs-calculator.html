Item details
  • Estimate daily energy needs with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and an activity factor.
  • Inputs: Weight (kg), Height (cm), Age, Sex adjustment (+5 male, −161 female), Activity factor (1.2 sedentary – 1.9 very active)
  • Results: BMR (kcal/day), Estimated daily needs (kcal)
  • Scenario table and chart for different activity factor (1.2 sedentary – 1.9 very active)
How to use it
  1. Enter your numbers in the yellow cells on 'Calculator'.
  2. Results update instantly on the right.
  3. Use 'Scenarios' (if included) to compare options side by side.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
