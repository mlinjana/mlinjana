Item details
  • Daily takings by channel and costs by category, with monthly profit.
  • 'Sales' log (300 rows): Date, Channel, Takings, Card fees
  • 'Costs' log (300 rows): Date, Supplier, Category, Amount
  • Automatic takings by channel for each month, with chart
  • Key numbers: Takings, Costs
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works
How to use it
  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Sales' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'Costs' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  5. Delete the example rows before you start.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
