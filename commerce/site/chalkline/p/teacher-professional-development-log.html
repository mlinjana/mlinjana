What's inside

  • Log courses, workshops and CPD points with hours, cost and certificates.
  • 'CPD' log (300 rows): Date, Activity, Provider, Type, Hours, Points, Cost…
  • Automatic hours totals by type, with chart
  • Key numbers: Hours, Points
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works

How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'CPD' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.
