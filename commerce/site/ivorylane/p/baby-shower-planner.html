How to use

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Guests' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'Budget' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
