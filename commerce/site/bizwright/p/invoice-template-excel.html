Invoice Template & Invoice Register

An auto-calculating invoice with a client drop-down, tax at your rate, and a register that flags overdue invoices.

  • XLSX

What's inside

  • Choose a client from a drop-down — address fills in automatically
  • Line items, subtotal, tax and total calculate
  • Due date from your payment terms
  • Invoice register with days overdue and outstanding total
  • Set tax to 0% if not registered

How to use it

  1. Open 'Settings' and enter your business name, contact details, tax label and rate, and payment details.
  2. Add your customers on the 'Clients' sheet (one row each).
  3. On the 'Invoice' sheet choose the client from the drop-down, set the invoice number and date, and add line items.
  4. Totals, tax and the due/valid-until date calculate automatically. Print or export to PDF (File → Save as PDF).
  5. Copy the key details into the 'Invoice Register' to track payment status and overdue days.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
