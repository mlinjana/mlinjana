How to use it

  1. Edit the income and expense categories on the 'Lists' sheet to suit your business.
  2. Record every transaction on 'Transactions' — date, type, category, description and amount.
  3. 'Summary' builds a month-by-month profit & loss and chart automatically for the year in cell B3.
  4. Delete the example rows (yellow cells) before you start.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
