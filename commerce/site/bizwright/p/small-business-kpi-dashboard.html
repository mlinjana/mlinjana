Item details
  • Monthly data sheet (36 months)
  • 10 KPIs: margins, growth, conversion, CAC, revenue per customer, cash
  • Month selector

How to use it
  1. Each month, enter your numbers on 'Monthly Data' (one row per month).
  2. 'Dashboard' calculates margins, growth, conversion and customer acquisition cost for the month you choose in B3.
  3. Set your targets on 'Monthly Data' row 'Target' to see green/red status.
Files and compatibility
