Item details
  • Complaints log with category, severity and status
  • Days-to-resolve and dashboard
  • Complaints by category chart
  • NPS tracker that groups promoters, passives and detractors
How to use it
  1. Log every complaint on 'Complaints' with category and severity; record the resolution date when closed.
  2. Days to resolve and the dashboard update automatically.
  3. Record survey scores (0–10) on 'NPS'. The Net Promoter Score = % promoters (9–10) − % detractors (0–6).
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
