What's inside

  • Map who is trained in what, spot gaps and plan training.
  • 'Matrix' log (300 rows): Employee, Skill, Level, Trained on, Refresher due
  • Automatic count by level, with chart
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works

How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Matrix' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.

Works with
