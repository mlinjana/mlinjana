Client CRM & Sales Pipeline Tracker

A simple spreadsheet CRM: track opportunities by stage, see weighted pipeline value and forecast by close month.

What's inside

  • 300-row pipeline with stage-based win probability
  • Weighted forecast by stage and by close month

How to use it

  1. Adjust pipeline stages and their win probabilities on 'Lists' if needed.
  2. Add each opportunity on 'Pipeline' with value, stage and expected close date.
  3. Weighted value = deal value × stage probability. 'Forecast' groups it by expected close month.
  4. Log calls, emails and meetings on 'Activity' so nothing falls through the cracks.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
