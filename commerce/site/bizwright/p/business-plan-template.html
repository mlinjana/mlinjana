How to use it

  1. Open the .docx file in Microsoft Word, Google Docs (File → Open → Upload) or LibreOffice Writer.
  2. Save a master copy, then replace the grey [bracketed guidance] with your own content.
  3. Delete any sections you don't need and export to PDF when you share it.
  4. Enter your assumptions on 'Assumptions' (yellow cells): starting revenue, growth, margins and fixed costs.
  5. 'Projections' calculates 36 months of revenue, gross profit, operating costs and net profit.
  6. 'Summary' totals each year and shows break-even month and the cash position.
  7. Paste the yearly summary into section 11 of the business plan.

Works with

Microsoft Word 2010+, Google Docs, LibreOffice Writer and Apple Pages (DOCX import). Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
