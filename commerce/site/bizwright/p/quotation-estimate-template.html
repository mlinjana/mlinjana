How to use

  1. Open 'Settings' and enter your business name, contact details, tax label and rate, and payment details.
  2. Add your customers on the 'Clients' sheet (one row each).
  3. On the 'Quote' sheet choose the client from the drop-down, set the quote number and date, and add line items.
  4. Totals, tax and the due/valid-until date calculate automatically. Print or export to PDF (File → Save as PDF).
  5. Copy the key details into the 'Quote Register' to track acceptance.
