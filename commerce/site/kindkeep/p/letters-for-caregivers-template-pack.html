How to use it
  1. Open the .docx file in Microsoft Word, Google Docs (File → Open → Upload) or LibreOffice Writer.
  2. Save a master copy, then replace the grey [bracketed guidance] with your own content.
  3. Delete any sections you don't need and export to PDF when you share it.
Files and compatibility
