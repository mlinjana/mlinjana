Item details
  • Record blood pressure, pulse and blood glucose readings with notes.
  • 'BP' log (300 rows): Date, Time, Systolic, Diastolic, Pulse, Notes
  • 'Glucose' log (300 rows): Date, Time, Reading (mmol/L), Notes
  • Key numbers: Average systolic, Average diastolic
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works
How to use it
  1. Read through the column headings so you know what each one records.
  2. Record entries on 'BP' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'Glucose' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  5. Delete the example rows before you start.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
