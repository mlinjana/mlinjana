What's inside

  • Track claims submitted, amounts paid and shortfalls so nothing is missed.
  • 'Claims' log (300 rows): Service date, Provider, Amount charged, Claim submitted, Paid by insurer, Shortfall, Status
  • Automatic amount charged totals by status, with chart
  • Key numbers: Charged, Shortfalls
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works

How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Claims' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.

Works with

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
