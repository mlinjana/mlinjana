Item details
  • Keep client details, service history, formulas, preferences and patch-test dates.
  • 'Clients' log (300 rows): Client, Phone, Birthday, Allergies / sensitivities, Patch test date, Preferences, Marketing consent?
  • 'History' log (300 rows): Date, Client, Service, Formula / products, Price, Stylist
  • Automatic price totals by service, with chart
  • Key numbers: Clients, Service revenue
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works
How to use it
  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Clients' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Record entries on 'History' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  4. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  5. Delete the example rows before you start.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
