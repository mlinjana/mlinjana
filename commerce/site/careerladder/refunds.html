Refunds & support

Career Ladder Kit sells digital downloads. Your files are available immediately after PayFast confirms your payment.

If something is wrong

If a file is faulty, doesn't match its description, or you can't open it with the software listed on the product page, contact us within 14 days of purchase with your order number. We'll help you get it working, send a corrected file, or refund you in full.

Change of mind

Because digital files can't be returned, we don't normally refund a change of mind once a file has been downloaded. If you bought the wrong product by mistake and haven't downloaded it, contact us and we'll help.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through PayFast. Your bank may take several working days to show it.

Lost download link

Links last 7 days and 5 downloads. If yours has expired, use the contact form with your order number and we'll send a new one.
