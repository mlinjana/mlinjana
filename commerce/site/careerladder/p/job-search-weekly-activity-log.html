Item details
  • Log applications, networking and learning every week and keep momentum.
  • 'Activity' log (300 rows): Date, Activity, Company / person, Minutes, Outcome
  • Automatic count by activity, with chart
  • Key numbers: Activities, Minutes
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works
How to use it
  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Activity' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.
Files and compatibility

Microsoft Excel 2010+, Excel for the web, Google Sheets and LibreOffice Calc. No macros.
