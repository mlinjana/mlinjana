What's inside

  • Record wins as they happen so reviews and promotions are easy to prepare.
  • 'Wins' log (300 rows): Date, Achievement, Category, Impact (number), Evidence / link
  • Automatic count by category, with chart
  • Editable drop-down lists, protected formulas, example rows to show how it works

How to use it

  1. Edit the drop-down options on the 'Lists' sheet to match your situation.
  2. Record entries on 'Wins' — one row each. Yellow cells are inputs; white cells calculate.
  3. Open 'Summary' to see totals, key numbers and the chart update automatically.
  4. Delete the example rows before you start.
