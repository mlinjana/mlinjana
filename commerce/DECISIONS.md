# DECISIONS

1. **One shared engine, three brands.** Static storefronts generated by one Python/Jinja2 builder from one catalogue; one Supabase backend. Near-zero hosting cost, no per-store servers.
2. **Deterministic product factory, no AI at run time.** Products come from code templates (reportlab/xlsxwriter/python-docx) + structured specs. Rebuilds are idempotent (spec + code hash checkpoint). AI was used once to author specs/content.
3. **Private IP stays out of the public repo.** The repo `mlinjana/mlinjana` is public, so `commerce/factory/` (generators) and `commerce/build/` (deliverables) are gitignored. Generators are backed up to the private `backups` bucket; files live in the private `products` bucket and are served only via 2-minute signed URLs after a verified payment.
4. **PayFast first.** SA-native, ZAR, public sandbox so checkout can be tested without your credentials. Paystack's published card rates are lower — a second adapter is a good next step.
5. **Order is paid only on a verified ITN:** signature (MD5 + passphrase) ✓, PayFast server validation = `VALID` ✓, merchant & mode match ✓, amount matches ✓. Idempotent `pending → paid` update.
6. **Vercel Hobby for staging only.** Its terms prohibit commercial use; live sales need Cloudflare Pages (free) or Vercel Pro (paid).
7. **Supabase pg_cron for scheduling** (runs without your computer or a Claude session). Jobs are capped per day; 5 consecutive errors or a budget breach flips a kill switch that pauses automation and checkout.
8. **Prices are evidence-based (2026-10-28) but untested by real sales.** `engine/pricing.py` places each product inside the typical band of observed competitor list prices (Etsy/Gumroad/Payhip/Flevy) — mid-market because the shops have no reviews; 52 of 60 benchmarked, 8 estimated by analogy (flagged). Own-store ZAR = USD × 16.4 × 0.75, rounded to R..9 (min R39). Full table with evidence: `research/pricing.csv`.
9. **Admin auth = owner key** (SHA-256 hash stored server-side, constant-time compare). Supabase Auth email links would need dashboard config not available to the connector.
10. **No cookies / no IP logging** in analytics: page path, product, referrer host only.
11. **Brand names:** Plainday Studio, Bizwright, Abacus Lane (domains available at check time; no conflicting brand found in web search; formal trademark search still required).
12. **Sandbox signing.** PayFast's shared sandbox merchant (10000100) rejected signatures made with the passphrase published in third-party docs, but accepts unsigned forms. In sandbox mode without a configured passphrase the form is unsigned and the ITN is trusted only after PayFast server validation (`VALID`) + merchant + amount checks. **Live mode always requires the signature** with your passphrase.
13. **Supabase client imported via `npm:` specifier** — the bundler timed out fetching from JSR twice.
14. **Deploy via Git integration, not file upload** — uploading the site through Claude would cost ~760 KB of text per update; Git deploys cost nothing.
15. **20 stores × 30 products (owner request, 2026-10-28).** 17 niche stores added (family, teachers, weddings/events, fitness, landlords, freelancers/creators, nonprofits, food businesses, online sellers, careers, trades, caregivers, growers, pets, beauty, transport, homeschool) and 10 more products for each original store → 600 products. Niches were chosen from the earlier opportunity research as audiences with recurring admin/planning needs that templates solve; demand is **not yet proven by sales** — expand or prune per store once real traffic and orders exist.
16. **Uniqueness rule.** Every product has a distinct purpose and structure; titles, slugs and IDs are unique across all 20 stores. Five near-duplicates of existing Bizwright/Abacus products found during review were replaced before building.
17. **Compact declarative specs.** New products are declared in one call each (`factory/gen.py`: spreadsheet log/tracker, calculator, printable PDF, Word template, bundle). Listing text (SEO title, 13 tags, features) is generated from the declaration — no AI at run time.
18. **Validation beyond "no error cells".** Besides LibreOffice recalculation (0 error cells), a sample of calculators is cross-checked against independently computed values; this caught and fixed a sign error in the Rent vs Buy calculator before release.
19. **Category-band pricing for the 540 new products.** Individual competitor benchmarks were researched only for the original 60. New products are priced by format and size inside the same researched bands (printables $3–6, simple trackers $5–10, Word templates $5–12; +20–30% for business-use niches; bundles ≈65% of parts). Every such price is labelled `estimate (category band)` in `research/pricing.csv`.
20. **Product images stay in the repo** (~39 MB for 600 products; my earlier ~180 MB estimate was wrong), so no separate media bucket or CSP change is needed.
21. **Parallel, checkpointed factory.** `run.py --jobs 4` builds in 4 processes (own LibreOffice profile each), bundles after their components; per-product `record.json` checkpoints. Full 600-product build ≈ 10 minutes.
22. **Scale fixes for 600 products:** health check fetches pages 20 at a time with batched status updates; reports and dashboard page through tables (PostgREST returns ≤1000 rows per request); sync upserts in chunks and reports errors.
23. **Store names reviewed (owner delegated the decision, 2026-10-28).** 16 of the 17 new names kept as they are (19 of 20 stores unchanged). **Pacebook Fitness → Steady Rep Studio** (`/steadyrep/`, old `/pacebook/` URLs redirect 301): no existing "Pacebook" brand was found, but the name is one letter from Facebook and not worth the trademark risk. Internal id `pacebook` and product IDs `PCB-xxx` are unchanged. No stores dropped before real traffic exists. **Pruning rule:** after 90 days live, a store with under 1% of network product views and no sales is paused and its catalogue reviewed.
24. **Live hosting: Cloudflare Pages (free).** I found no commercial-use ban (secondary sources only). Its limits fit this site (20,000 files per site against ~2,900 here; 500 builds a month). Netlify's free plan was rejected: it has a hard 300-credit monthly cap, and one source prices each production deploy at 15 credits. The site now ships Cloudflare `_headers`/`_redirects` matching `vercel.json`. Moving requires the owner to connect Cloudflare (account creation is the owner's).
25. **Owner email before a domain exists:** Resend's test sender can mail the account owner's own address, which is enough for the daily report. Customer emails wait for a verified domain. I chose not to run a daily Claude session just to email the report, to keep Claude usage low.
26. **Spending stays off until you approve an amount.** No domains (one network domain is recommended first, not 20), no Etsy listing fees and no paid plans. "Take authority" was not treated as approval of open-ended spending.
27. **Build hashes include each store's brand data**, so a rename or colour change rebuilds that store's files (caught when the first rename attempt skipped the 30 fitness products).
28. **Live host is a Cloudflare Worker serving static assets** (`commerce-network.mlinjanachuma.workers.dev`), not a Pages project: the dashboard Git setup was confusing on mobile, and the connected Cloudflare connector can't deploy. Claude deploys with Wrangler and a Workers-scoped API token kept in the git-ignored `.env`. Cloudflare drops `.html` from page addresses with a 307 redirect and keeps query strings; the sandbox purchase confirmed the payment return still works. Vercel now only redirects (308) to the live host, so no real sale can happen on Vercel's non-commercial Hobby plan.
29. **Seller entity: Mlinjana Capital Holdings (Pty) Ltd** (reg. 2026/067820/07), chosen by the owner, 2026-10-28. Each store is presented as a trading name of the company, in the footer and on the Terms and Privacy pages. The address is published exactly as the owner gave it; no city or postal code was added by guesswork. The phone number is not published; contact is through the form.
30. **Store Manager = scheduled Claude sessions plus the always-on backend.** Checkout, delivery, health checks and the daily report already run around the clock (Supabase and Cloudflare). On top of that: one short daily manager run (orders, support, owner email) and one weekly growth run (SEO content, conversion, pricing rule). These aren't continuous agents: each run uses the owner's Claude plan, so the runs are kept short. Both follow OPERATIONS.md hard rules: no spending, no accounts, no fake claims, no unsolicited contact. Paid ads and new marketplace or social accounts still need the owner.
31. **Storefront redesign (owner request, 29 Oct 2026): "real stores, not AI-generated looking".** Each store gets its own display typeface (6 self-hosted OFL fonts), an animated backdrop generated from its colours, a 3D fan of real product covers in the opening section, cards that tilt toward the pointer, scroll-in animations, a category ticker, a 3D document viewer with full-screen zoom, a sticky buy bar on phones, "how it works", who makes the products, and FAQs. 3D is pure CSS (no WebGL library) to keep pages light on mobile data. All motion turns off under prefers-reduced-motion. No fake reviews, counters or urgency: authenticity comes from showing the real pages inside each product.
32. **Product artwork v2:** covers and previews now crop to the real content and zoom in, so tables, charts and pages are legible. They're set in the store's typeface on soft colour fields, with a layered page stack. Bundles lead with their products. Fixed: bundle listings now show every format they contain (previously only the bonus file's PDF).
33. **Network domain: mlinjanastudios.com** (owner registered it at Cloudflare Registrar, 29 Oct 2026, on Claude's recommendation). It matches the seller company, one domain covers all 20 stores (/store/), and it carries low trademark risk. The Worker serves the domain, www. and the old workers.dev address. `workers_dev: true` is set explicitly: adding custom domains had silently switched workers.dev off for about 2 minutes, and that was fixed at once. Canonical tags point at the domain. Next: customer receipt emails need Resend domain verification (DNS records), and hello@ forwarding needs Cloudflare Email Routing. Both need permissions the current Workers-only token doesn't have.

34. **Marketplace redesign (owner request, 5 Nov 2026: "take inspiration from Etsy, Takealot, Shein").** mlinjanastudios.com becomes one marketplace: a site-wide search across every product, a department bar, Etsy-style product cards with a favourites heart (saved on the visitor's device only), and calmer product pages (gallery, price, one clear Buy button, details in sections, "meet the shop"). The 20 stores become shops inside it and keep their own colours and typeface on their shop pages. The 3D/tilt effects from #31 are dropped in favour of a clean, trustworthy layout. Nothing from those sites is copied (no names, logos or colours), and none of their urgency tactics (countdowns, "x in baskets", fake ratings) are used. Etsy and Shein block automated visitors, so the design follows their widely known patterns; Takealot's home page was viewed directly.
35. **Demand-led catalogue expansion (owner request, 5 Nov 2026: "only products people want").** Evidence and build order: research/DEMAND.md. Owner decisions: build **English first** (Afrikaans and isiZulu only after review by a fluent speaker); offer **a few genuinely free downloads** to reach people searching "free …" (no email required), with paid versions that do more; include **funeral and unveiling programmes, Bible study and faith (public-domain verse text only), and seniors and dementia activity packs (no medical claims)**.
36. **Storefront v3 and basket checkout (owner request, 5 Nov 2026: "clean like Takealot and Shein, trusted at first glance, converts").** Light grey canvas with white panels, one sans-serif family (Plus Jakarta Sans), a proper logo mark, a utility bar, a department mega-menu, a promo carousel, product carousels, a trust strip, and a three-column product page with a buy box. All 600 product images were re-rendered in one clean catalogue style: real pages on paper or in a laptop mockup, on a neutral background, with no text baked in. Product files are unchanged (verified: identical hashes and prices). **Basket:** customers can buy several items in one PayFast payment. Each item stays its own order row (same delivery, tokens and reports); a new `baskets` row holds the payment (migration 004, store-api v7). Verified end to end in the PayFast sandbox: BSK-43FF71663A5B (2 items, R78, fee split per item, both ZIPs downloaded) and single order ORD-76F447C8B791. Trust signals are factual only: registered company details, PayFast checkout, the 14-day file guarantee from the refund policy, and no ratings, countdowns or "x people viewing".
37. **Logo and brand colours (owner, 5 Nov 2026).** After two rounds (seven original concepts, shown on business cards, a phone home screen, a browser tab and a social profile), the owner chose **D · Folded page**: a bold M on a page whose corner folds over in gold. The wordmark's i and j dots are gold squares. Palette kept: **Emerald #0B6B57 and Marigold #F5B301**, with Ink, Slate, Mist, Mint and Signal Red. Rules and files: brand/BRAND.md. All artwork is original and generated as vectors in brand/make_logo.py. A trademark search (CIPC) hasn't been done; do it before registering the mark.
38. **One seller, category-led marketplace (owner, 5 Nov 2026: "like Takealot and Amazon: we can't put the stores on the front").** Customers now see only **Mlinjana Studios** as the seller. The 20 studios are internal product lines: on the site each one is a plain category (config/departments.json → "collections", e.g. Chairbook Studio → "Salon & Beauty Business"). Cards show the product's category, product pages say "Sold and supported by Mlinjana Studios", and the home page has "Shop by category". Single-category departments point their canonical URL at the department page. URLs (/chairbook/…) are unchanged so checkout return links and old links keep working. **Downloads:** every PDF, spreadsheet and Word file now names Mlinjana Studios (footers, file properties, PDF covers with the logo), with Mlinjana Capital Holdings (Pty) Ltd as copyright holder. READ ME FIRST.txt and LICENSE.txt are replaced by a designed two-page "Start Here.pdf" (guide and plain-language licence). **Receipts:** branded HTML email with a plain-text alternative, sent as "Mlinjana Studios" (store-api v8). Sending from @mlinjanastudios.com, and delivering to any customer, needs the domain verified at Resend (DNS records, owner step).
39. **Mlinjana Studios 2.0 decisions (owner, 6 Nov 2026).** Discovery report: https://claude.ai/artifact/TQi3EcD51Ynpbq9QGrKa1F. (a) **Palette: Midnight Navy #17243B, Warm Ivory #F8F6F0, Champagne Gold #C9952A, Charcoal #252A32, White**, replacing #37's emerald and marigold. The folded-page logo shape stays and is recoloured. Gold is not used for small text on ivory or white (about 2.5:1 contrast). (b) **Technology: keep and extend** the static site (Python/Jinja) and Supabase rather than rebuild in Next.js, so the 600 products, URLs, checkout and delivery keep working. (c) **Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics plus Google Search Console.** No GA4, so no cookie banner is needed. Cloudflare's beacon is allowed in the CSP, and the privacy policy names it. (d) **Licences: three simple tiers.** Personal and Business at today's price. Team (up to 10 people in one organisation) at 2.5×. Commercial/Enterprise by quote. **Addresses:** links and canonical tags use the extensionless form Cloudflare serves (e.g. /chairbook/p/slug). Old .html links keep working through Cloudflare's 307 redirect, which preserves the query string.
40. **Stage 2 design goes to a preview first (6 Nov 2026).** The navy/ivory/gold design, the Business / Life / Education menu (config/taxonomy.json: all 600 products mapped into 3 divisions and 29 categories) and the static "Make Better Things Happen." hero run on a separate Worker, https://mlinjana-preview.mlinjanachuma.workers.dev. It is noindex, uses the PayFast sandbox and has no custom domain. The live site keeps the build from commit 34ce523. `engine/deploy_cloudflare.sh` refuses to deploy while `.preview-only` exists, unless CONFIRM_LIVE=1 is set after the owner approves. Product and shop URLs don't change. The old /c/<department>/ pages still answer but are no longer linked. At go-live they get 301 redirects to their closest division or category. **Go-live checklist:** owner approval of the look and the category names; recolour the receipt email (store-api, sandbox end-to-end test before deploy) and Start Here.pdf in all 600 downloads (factory re-render, SHA-checked sync); add the /c/ 301s; remove `.preview-only`; deploy; re-run the link check and Lighthouse on the live site.
41. **2.0 go-live preparation (owner approved the preview: "Proceed", 6 Nov 2026).** Done: store-api v9 (navy/gold receipt; short PayFast return and cancel URLs; sandbox single and basket purchases passed, receipt delivery untested until the Resend domain is verified). All 600 downloads rebuilt with the navy Start Here.pdf and logo: 600/600 validated, uploaded, and the database file fingerprint matches the catalogue. Names, prices and URLs are unchanged. Factory backup `factory/factory-2026-11-06-navy.zip`. Old /c/ department pages now 301 to their 2.0 category or division. `.preview-only` removed. The weekly task's stale-copy check now requires the 2.0 files, so it can't redeploy the old design. The owner then confirmed the production deploy explicitly ("Deploy now"). **Live on mlinjanastudios.com on 6 Nov 2026** (Cloudflare version 53c256f4).
42. **Licence tiers built (decision #39d, 6 Nov 2026).** Standard covers you, your household and one business, at the listed price. Team covers up to 10 people in one organisation (one company, school, church or non-profit), at 2.5× the listed price **rounded to the nearest whole rand** (R39 → R98, R59 → R148). Rounding keeps prices in whole rand like the rest of the catalogue. Commercial/enterprise use (more than 10 people, several organisations, or the files inside products the buyer sells) is quoted by hand through a prefilled contact form, and never reaches checkout. Only store-api computes the charged price; the browser shows the same rule for display. Each order row stores its licence (migration 005). The receipt names the licence and says it is the buyer's proof of licence. Start Here.pdf's licence page explains Standard and Team, and points to the quote form. **The licence wording is plain-language and was not written by a lawyer:** an attorney should review /help/licence and Start Here.pdf before real sales start.
